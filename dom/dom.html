Spider Man

About

Spider-Man is a superhero appering in American comic books published by Marvel Comics Created by writer-editor Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko,he first appeared in the anthology comic books Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962) in the Silver Age of Comic Books. He was been featured in comic books, television shows,films,video games, novels, and plays
